Mediterranean cooking, Nairobi pace

Geco Café brings a Mediterranean and café menu to a garden setting — flatbreads, pizza from the oven, and plates built around vegetables, olive oil and citrus. Vegetarian options run through the whole menu, not just a side column. The terrace is easy to work from in the morning and easy to linger at come evening, with parking at the door and Wi-Fi throughout. Geco is also known for hosting live band performances — food and beyond.
